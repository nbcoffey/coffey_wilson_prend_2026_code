These datasets are third-party products redistributed solely for the reproducibility of Coffey, Wilson & Prend (2026), JGR: Oceans.

They are not primary sources. Please cite the original sources below.

- orsi_fronts/ (saf.txt, pf.txt, sbdy.txt, saccf.txt, stf.txt): Antarctic Circumpolar Current frontal positions from Orsi, Whitworth & Nowlin (1995), Deep-Sea Research I, 42(5), 641-673, doi:10.1016/0967-0637(95)00021-W.

- dot/Full_DOT_data_Anto.nc: Southern Ocean dynamic ocean topography from Armitage, Kwok, Thompson & Cunningham (2018), JGR: Oceans, doi:10.1002/2017JC013534. The original distribution link (rkwok.jpl.nasa.gov/cryosat) is no longer accessible (last checked in 2026).
